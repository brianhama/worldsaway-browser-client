A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

WorldsAway Help

Click Open an object’s context menu.

Double click Walk to a place in the scene.

↑ ↓ ← → Walk while the scene has focus.

Enter Focus the conversation input.

I Open your pockets.

M Open your mail.

Esc Close a dialog.

Start as a ghost? Open Avatar and choose Materialize to take physical form. Click an object to open its context menu.

Use Preferences → Animate world to control idle animations, gestures and smooth walking. Your system’s reduced motion setting is used until you save a preference.

Devices, documents, housing, appearance and building controls are available through their object's actions. Tools lets you search every recovered action and view replies.

Browser conversion is under development. All known wire requests are available; original Tcl-driven presentation and exact animation parity are not yet verified. No server-provided scripts are executed in the page.
